Get COOK onto Cookie Chain.

Native COOK on Cookie Chain is the SVM native mint. Bridged COOK on Solana is a Token-2022 mint. The Hyperlane warp route moves them 1:1 under a community multi-sig.

  1. Install Nightly and create/import a Solana wallet.
  2. Buy COOK on Solana (`36ZrtQoab5MhhySaP1YSTwUahSk6GRVUTtZ6cuVfm9e1`).
  3. Open the Cookie Chain Bridge and send Solana → Cookie.
  4. In Nightly, switch RPC to https://rpc.cookiescan.io (The Oven does this for you on connect).
  5. Come back here and bake.
